Connect your systems

Your website and business tools pass details to each other on their own, and only genuine requests get through.

What you get

  • A new booking, order or enquiry reaches the right tool straight away, with no copying and pasting.
  • Anything that didn't come from your own systems is turned away, so fake or altered requests never get in.
  • One small connection, scoped to the job, tested and handed over.

How it works

  1. Something happens on your website: a booking, an order or an enquiry.
  2. The details are sent straight to your other tool, sealed so it can tell they really came from you.
  3. Your tool checks the seal. Genuine details go through; anything else is turned away and nothing changes.

Good fits

  • Website enquiries landing in your inbox or customer list
  • Online bookings or orders updating your calendar or stock
  • Payment notices reaching your accounts tool
