Connect your systems

Your website and business tools pass details to each other on their own, and only genuine requests get through.

How it works

  1. Your shop, booking tool or form sends a short message each time something happens.
  2. Each message carries a seal made with a key only your systems share.
  3. This connection checks the seal first. A good seal is accepted straight away. A missing or broken seal is refused, and nothing is passed on.

What you get

  • A new booking, order or enquiry reaches the right tool straight away, with no copying and pasting.
  • Anything that didn't come from your own systems is turned away, so forged or altered requests never get in.
  • One small connection, scoped to the job, tested and handed over.
